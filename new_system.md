1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
This Software Requirements Specification outlines a comprehensive digital platform designed to bridge the gap between academic faculty knowledge and contemporary industry practices within the state polytechnic system. The Faculty Training Calendar and Development Portal transforms ad-hoc professional development into a structured, accountable, and outcome-driven system that directly benefits student learning through practical, job-relevant curriculum integration.
The system addresses a systemic challenge: faculty operate with theoretical frameworks while industry evolves rapidly, creating a disconnect that disadvantages graduating students. By establishing institutional capacity through structured faculty training, digital tracking, verified attendance, and mandatory lesson plan integration, this initiative ensures continuous knowledge transfer from industry to students.

2. PROJECT BACKGROUND & CONTEXT
2.1 Institutional Context
The polytechnic system comprises six engineering disciplines: - Mechanical Engineering - Electrical Engineering - Electronics & Communication Engineering - Civil Engineering - Information Technology (IT) - Computer Science
2.2 Problem Statement
Current Challenges: - Faculty training is ad-hoc, unscheduled, and lacks institutional visibility - No structured mechanism to track which faculty have received industry exposure - Limited accountability for translating learning into student-facing curriculum changes - Disconnect between industry-relevant skills and classroom instruction - No data-driven insights to refine training policies or evaluate training partner effectiveness - Students graduate with theoretical knowledge but insufficient hands-on expertise
2.3 Solution Overview
A comprehensive digital ecosystem that combines: 1. Whole-year training calendar published and accessible to all faculty 2. Transparent digital application process for training enrollment 3. Real-time, time-stamped attendance verification 4. Structured post-training feedback collection 5. Mandatory lesson plan creation integrating new learning into curriculum 6. Administrative dashboards for institutional analysis and decision-making

3. PRODUCT SCOPE & FEATURES
3.1 Core Functional Features
3.1.1 Training Calendar Management
Purpose: Provide institution-wide visibility of available faculty training opportunities throughout the academic year.
Features: - 
Core Calendar Functionality The annual training calendar displays all scheduled training sessions across all six disciplines and is accessible to all authenticated faculty users.
Session Information Each training session includes the following details: session title, description, target discipline(s), instructor/partner details, dates, times, duration, location (physical/hybrid/online), capacity limits, pre-requisites, and expected learning outcomes.
Search and Filtering The calendar supports filtering by discipline, date range, training partner, delivery mode, and difficulty level. An advanced search functionality allows users to search by keyword, trainer, skill area, and industry partner.

3.1.2 Digital Training Application Process
Purpose: Create transparent, documented, and auditable faculty training enrollment while reducing administrative overhead.
Features: - 
Faculty Self-Service Portal Faculty users can browse and apply for training sessions through a self-service portal. The application form captures applicant details, the training session selected, relevance to teaching discipline, expected application of learning, and supervisor approval (as required or optional per institutional policy).
Application Tracking and Notifications Real-time application status tracking displays the current state of each submission: submitted, pending review, approved, rejected, or waitlisted. Automated email notifications are sent to applicants at each status change to keep them informed throughout the process.
Administrative Review and Approval Administrators and Coordinators can review, approve, or reject applications and provide feedback comments. The system allows coordinators to set application deadlines per session (for example, 2 weeks before training) to manage timely enrollment.

3.1.3 Verifiable Attendance Tracking
Purpose: Ensure objective, timestamped attendance records eliminate informal/paper-based tracking and establish institutional accountability.
Features: - 
Digital Attendance Marking Time-stamped digital attendance marking occurs on the training day
Real-Time Monitoring A real-time attendance dashboard is visible to trainers and coordinators, allowing them to track attendance as sessions progress and identify any issues immediately.
Post-Session Reporting Automated attendance reports are generated following each training session, documenting participation and engagement metrics for administrative review.
Certificates and Completion Attendance certificates are generated upon completion.
Lesson Plan Linkage linked to lesson plan submission requirements, ensuring that training completion and application of learning are properly documented.
3.1.4 Structured Training Feedback System
Purpose: Collect data-driven qualitative and quantitative insights to refine training policies and evaluate training partner effectiveness.
Features: - 
Features
Post-Training Feedback Collection. The feedback form can capture multiple dimensions of the training experience through both quantitative ratings and qualitative responses.
Quantitative Feedback Components The feedback form includes several rating scales to measure different aspects of training effectiveness. Faculty rate the relevance of training to their teaching discipline on a 1-5 scale, evaluate the overall quality of the trainer and content, assess how directly applicable the learning is to their students, and rate whether stated learning outcomes were achieved.
Qualitative Feedback Components Open-ended questions capture key takeaways, suggestions for improvement, and topics faculty would like to see in future training.
Feedback Management Feedback response rates are tracked and reported to identify participation patterns across departments and faculty segments. 

3.1.5 Learning Transfer & Lesson Plan Integration
Purpose: Ensure training insights are actively integrated into curriculum, directly benefiting students and validating training ROI.
Features: - 
Mandatory Lesson Plan Submission Faculty are required to submit a lesson plan following training completion, with submission due within 2–4 weeks after training ends..
Structured Lesson Plan Template A comprehensive lesson plan template guides faculty in translating training into curriculum. The template requires mapping learning objectives to training content, identifying new skills and technologies being introduced to students, describing classroom delivery methods (lectures, labs, projects, demonstrations), and detailing hands-on activities that leverage industry-aligned content.
Quality Review and Feedback Coordinators and subject matter experts conduct quality assessments of submitted lesson plans. Constructive feedback encourages continuous improvement and helps faculty strengthen their curriculum integration efforts.

1. Faculty Member Portal Features
A. Training Discovery & Calendar
•	Unified Calendar View: Access a whole-year training calendar displaying sessions across all six engineering disciplines.
•	Multi-View Options: Switch between Month (planning), Week (overview), and List (chronological) views .
•	Advanced Search & Filtering: Filter sessions by discipline, date range, partner, delivery mode, and difficulty. Search by keyword, trainer, or skill area.
•	Visual Indicators: View color-coded sessions by discipline and status badges (e.g., "Available", "Full").
•	Detailed Session Previews: Click any session to view outcomes, prerequisites, trainer bio, capacity, and location details .
B. Application & Enrollment
•	Self-Service Application: Apply for training directly through the portal.
•	Application Form: Input details regarding the session's relevance to teaching and expected application of learning.
•	Status Tracking: Track application status in real-time (Submitted, Pending, Approved, Rejected, Waitlisted).
•	Notifications: Receive automated email alerts when application status changes.
C. Attendance & Certification
•	Digital Attendance Marking: Mark attendance digitally on the day of training with time-stamping.
•	Certificates: Download attendance certificates automatically generated upon completion.
D. Post-Training Activities
•	Feedback Submission: Submit structured feedback on trainer quality, content relevance, and industry alignment using rating scales and open-ended questions .
•	Lesson Plan Integration: Access a structured template to create lesson plans that map training objectives to student learning outcomes.
•	Submission Portal: Upload mandatory lesson plans within 2–4 weeks of training completion.
•	Feedback Receipt: Receive constructive feedback on submitted lesson plans from coordinators.
